Kirowen is an open, free registry of immutable events. Independent systems send it events about their own objects. Kirowen puts every event into a single global sequence and gives anyone cryptographic proof that the history has not been changed.

    →

    Append-only

    Events are never updated or deleted. A correction is a new event.

    ◆

    Tamper-evident

    The history is a Merkle transparency log (RFC 6962, C2SP). If someone rewrites it, including the operator, anyone who kept a proof can detect it.

    #

    Hash-only

    You send a signed envelope with a hash of your data, never the data itself. Your content stays with you.

    ∅

    Free, no token

    No blockchain, no cryptocurrency, no fees. Open standards and open source (Apache 2.0).

    What it guarantees, and what it does not

    Kirowen proves that a source signed a statement and that the statement has not been altered since it entered the log. Tampering is detected, not made impossible: detection relies on verifiers holding proofs and on the log being watched. Kirowen does not say whether a statement is true, and this alpha runs on a single server with no availability guarantee.

    Register a source

    A source is you, or a system of yours, that records events. Registering is free and needs no account.

    1. Your browser creates an Ed25519 key and signs the registration. The key never leaves your browser; the registry only sees its public half.
    2. You get a token to call the API. It is shown only once.
    3. Download your files. With them you can use the API console, your own code or the kirowen CLI.

    Registering adds a permanent entry to the public log. Limit: 5 registrations per IP per hour.
